Creamy Garlic Pasta

A delicious and creamy garlic pasta that is simple to prepare and perfect for a quick homemade meal.

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 cup fresh cream
  • ½ cup grated cheese
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Fresh parsley

Preparation Steps

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water until soft.
  2. Melt butter in a pan and sauté minced garlic.
  3. Add fresh cream and mix well.
  4. Add cheese, salt and black pepper.
  5. Add the cooked pasta and combine everything.
  6. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve hot.
